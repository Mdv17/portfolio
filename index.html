Case study 04 · Multi-market creative

One product, five markets

SED sold the same products through Johannesburg, Port Elizabeth and Lusaka, into Zimbabwe, and through resellers. I localised every ad: currency, contacts, address and offer. That kept the brand consistent and made each one feel local.

For Black Friday 2025 I packaged the range into 13 kits matched to buyer profiles, from renters (PS3000 portable, R32,999) to large homes (6kW premium hybrid, R194,999), and designed the launch creative for each market.
